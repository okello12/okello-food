## Name story: precise interpretation

- Guaraní *tembi'u* = food/delicacy (https://guaraniayvu.org/ ; English and Spanish dictionary entries).
- Swahili *chakula* = food or meal (https://wold.clld.org/word/72141734846459395 ; University of Dar es Salaam TUKI dictionary).
- **Tembika is a coined brand combining syllables/letters**, not a direct translation of food or a recognised word in either language. The trailing "ka" is a sound taken from *chakula*, not an independently established term meaning food.

## Preliminary exact-name usage and possible conflicts

- Web search returned no obvious existing *nutrition-tracking application* exactly called Tembika. This is limited negative web-search evidence, **not proof of ownership or clearance**.
- Exact uppercase term **TEMBIKA** is used for experimental tempe/food production in Indonesia. IPB University-associated community work on tempe made from rubber-tree seeds was reported as **TEMBIKA** in West Sumatra on 26 and 27 August 2026: https://padek.jawapos.com/amp/dharmasraya/2608260039/biji-karet-disulap-jadi-tempe-dharmasraya-siapkan-sentra-produksi-tembika and https://www.kliksiar.com/bimtek-tembika-dorong-pemanfaatan-biji-karet-menjadi-produk-pangan-bernilai-tambah-di-dharmasraya/
- A university document identifies a 2014 entrepreneurship project "**Tempe Biji Nangka (Tembika)**" using jackfruit seeds: https://lpmpp.trunojoyo.ac.id/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/data-prestasi-mahasiswa.pdf
- These are relevant exact-name food-sector uses, though not proven registered trade marks or direct software competitors. Risks depend on territory, prior rights, trading scale and product/services overlap.
- The Malay word **tembikai** means watermelon. It is a related-looking ordinary vocabulary term, not evidence of a trade mark conflict; don't conflate it with Tembika.
- No verified live RDAP/registrar result was obtained for **tembika.com**, **tembika.co.uk** or **tembika.app**. Do not state that the domains are free.
