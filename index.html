SMART PORTIONS

Built around your eating pattern

The suggestion engine reserves roughly 30% of your calories for lunch, 45% for dinner, 15% for a snack and 10% as flexibility. It also respects what you have already logged that day and the type of food you are adding.

A smart portion is a practical starting suggestion, not a requirement. Your scale entry always wins.

BACKUP

IPHONE INSTALL

Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Open the hosted app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open it from the new Okello Food icon.

Once installed, the app can work offline. Your logs and saved recipes remain on this device.

NOTE

Nutrition figures are practical estimates, not laboratory measurements. Mixed dishes from every cuisine can vary sharply by recipe, especially oil-heavy meals, curries, soups, stews, rice dishes and takeaways. Use packet labels, barcode data or your own recipe calculator where you can.
