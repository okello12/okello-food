Beta privacy and device-storage notice

Version 1 · 8 September 2026

Pre-launch notice. Okello Food is currently an adult beta. The operator's final legal name, postal address and privacy contact must be inserted here before any broad commercial launch. This notice describes what the current software actually does.

What stays on your device

Your food diary, targets, recipes, custom foods, weight history, activity, favourites, satiety feedback, first-run preferences and beta feedback are stored in this browser's local storage. There is no Okello Food account and no Okello Food cloud diary. The project operator cannot remotely open your local diary.

Local browser data can be lost if you clear website data, remove the browser profile, or lose/change the device. Use the app's complete or encrypted backup before changing phones or clearing browser data.

What can leave your device

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages serves the app. Like other web hosts, it can receive ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details and requested files.
  • Open Food Facts: when you deliberately look up a barcode or choose an online packaged-product search, the barcode or search term is sent to Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts can also receive ordinary request information such as your IP address. Product images, where shown, can be loaded from Open Food Facts.
  • Barcode library: the app downloads the open-source html5-qrcode library from jsDelivr and tries to cache it for later offline use.
  • Manual beta sharing: beta feedback remains on the device unless you export it and choose to send the exported file to somebody.

Okello Food does not currently use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

Voice entry

The beta does not start the browser Web Speech recognition service. You can still use the microphone built into your phone's keyboard if you choose. Keyboard dictation is provided by your device/browser platform and is outside Okello Food's control; check your device provider's privacy settings if you use it.

Why the app uses local storage

The diary cannot work offline or remember your entries without durable device storage. The app uses local storage for those user-requested functions and for update/recovery state. It does not use local storage for advertising.

Health-adjacent information

Food, weight, activity, targets and satiety can reveal information about health or fitness. Okello Food deliberately keeps the diary local in this beta and does not ask for medical conditions. Do not enter diagnoses, medication instructions or other clinical information into free-text fields.

Retention and deletion

Local diary data remains until you delete it, clear browser website data or remove it through the app. Beta feedback is capped locally by the feedback module. Backup files remain wherever you choose to save them. Settings includes a control to delete Okello Food's local data from this browser.

Your access and control

You can inspect your diary in the app and export a complete backup. Because the project operator does not hold your local diary on a server, the operator cannot remotely retrieve, correct or delete that local copy for you. If you separately send the operator an exported feedback or backup file, that shared copy becomes information the recipient holds and must be handled accordingly.

Children

This pre-launch beta is for adults aged 18 or over. It is not designed for children's weight management or dietary treatment.

Third parties and licences

Open Food Facts database content is used under the Open Database License (ODbL); individual database contents and product images have their own Open Food Facts licensing terms. See Third-party notices.

Medical boundary

Okello Food is for food literacy and general wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor or manage a disease, calculate medication or insulin doses, or replace a doctor or registered dietitian. See the in-app nutrition notes and beta terms.

Contact

Until the operating entity and formal privacy contact are finalised, project questions can be raised with the repository owner through the project's GitHub repository. A formal operator identity and privacy contact is a launch blocker and must be added before commercial launch.
