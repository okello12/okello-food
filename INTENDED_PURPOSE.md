# Okello Food intended purpose

Version 1 · 8 September 2026

## Intended purpose

Okello Food is an adult food-literacy and general-wellbeing application. It helps adults record meals, describe culturally familiar portions, calculate home-cooked recipes, inspect source-backed food information and understand approximate energy and nutrient totals.

It is designed to support everyday food understanding. It is not intended to diagnose, prevent, monitor, predict, prognose, treat or alleviate disease or injury, and it is not intended to provide clinical decision support.

## Intended users

Pre-launch beta: adults aged 18 or over.

The current beta is not designed for children, pregnancy nutrition care, eating-disorder treatment, or condition-specific diet management.

## Explicitly out of scope

Without a separate regulatory and clinical decision, Okello Food must not add features whose intended purpose is to:

- diagnose a disease or health condition;
- calculate insulin, medication or supplement doses;
- recommend starting, stopping or changing medication;
- manage diabetes, chronic kidney disease, hypertension or another named condition;
- create condition-specific therapeutic sodium, potassium, protein, carbohydrate or fluid prescriptions;
- detect deterioration or create clinical alerts;
- provide pregnancy-specific treatment guidance;
- produce clinician-facing diagnostic or treatment recommendations;
- calculate individual disease-risk scores presented for medical decision-making.

A disclaimer does not override an objectively medical intended purpose. Any proposal in the above categories requires an explicit regulatory review before implementation.

## Product language

Preferred language: food literacy, estimate, reference amount, logged amount, source, confidence, general wellbeing, habit projection.

Avoid unsupported language such as diagnose, treat, prescribe, clinically proven, medically recommended, safe for [condition], or manage your [condition].
