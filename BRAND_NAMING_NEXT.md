# New commercial brand shortlist — 21 November 2026

**Decision:** Portuva withdrawn at founder's instruction after finding Portiva: Calorie & Macro Coach, a close-spelling competitor. Bowlful previously withdrawn from first place after existing food-sector uses. **No new name is adopted or cleared.** Live beta remains Okello Food; do not rename shell, GitHub repo, app manifest, Stripe merchant or price/checkout, or register a domain yet.

## Current candidates for more serious clearance

1. **Porumi** (poh-roo-mee): short, approachable, more adaptable to a worldwide food diary and potentially a future nutritional data offering. Initial web search surfaced no exact-match live nutrition tracker. But Companies House lists PORUMI LIMITED (company 08819385) as **dissolved 4 Aug 2015**; historical Porumi handmade skincare usage exists in London. Dissolution does *not* extinguish unregistered or trade mark rights. Official UKIPO, WIPO, apps, commercial usage and live domains not exhaustively checked. Evidence: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/08819385 and https://porumi.weebly.com/ .
2. **Bowlari** (bowl-ah-ree): clearer food association, no indexed exact nutrition tracker in initial queries. Yet **Bolari** is used by a rice/food brand in France, including long-grain rice, potentially creating phonetic/confusion questions in food-related markets. Evidence: https://www.transgourmet.fr/restauration-commerciale/marques-nationales/bolari--BD_BOLARI.html and https://www.transgourmet.fr/restauration-commerciale/produit/riz-long-etuve-bolari--637264.html . The similarity is not an automatic legal bar; avoid calling Bowlari cleared.

## Required before adoption
- Official UKIPO exact and phonetic search in relevant classes 9, 42 and food/nutrition classes where needed: https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark .
- WIPO Global Brand Database similarity and territory checks; check international app usage, Apple App Store and Google Play, UK company/business names and common-law trading names: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/check-availability .
- **Live registrar/domain availability not established.** Independently check .com, .co.uk and .app plus relevant social handles before purchase.
- Take professional advice if any plausible close match. No inference that an absence from indexed web search means legal availability.

Recommended research priority: **Porumi first**, Bowlari second, subject to the founder actually choosing a replacement after clearance. Keep the existing name on the live app until then.
