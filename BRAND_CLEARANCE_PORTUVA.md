# Portuva: provisional naming decision and conflict assessment

**21 November 2026. Internal evidence note. NOT a trade mark clearance opinion.**

**RETIRED name** as of 21 November 2026: founder elected to change away from Portuva following the direct-sector near-name collision. This page is retained as evidence of why the name was withdrawn. Former pronunciation "por-too-vah". The decision records preference only, **not authorisation to launch, buy domains, rename the production app or create a live Stripe payment link**. Continue public beta as Okello Food pending clearance.

## Significant near-name collision

"Portiva: Calorie & Macro Coach" is marketed on the Apple App Store as an iPhone food/calorie tracker and is described by its operator at https://portiva.app/ . Its App Store copy describes barcode, photo and text food logging, world cuisine, cups/bowls/slices rather than grams, meal-recognition prompts, targets and premium plans. This is squarely within our planned nutrition sector and the spelling **Portuva** differs from **Portiva** in a single letter.

Primary references found online on 21 Nov 2026:
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/portiva-calorie-macro-coach/id6818309045
- https://portiva.app/

This is a **material commercial confusion risk**, not proof of a trade mark registration, prior legal rights in the UK, passing off, infringement or legal prohibition. Assessment must consider pronunciation, goods/services, mark status, territories and relevant users, and may require an IP professional.

## Exact-match web discovery

An initial broad web search did not surface an established standalone nutrition business **under exact spelling Portuva**. This does not establish freedom to use. The similarly spelled **Portiva** app was not identified in the first naming shortlist; that initial screening was incomplete. An unrelated historical internet username uses Portuva. Avoid treating a weak exact-match search as a negative clearance certificate.

Search official UKIPO marks for Portuva / Portiva and similar sound/spelling, class 9 (downloadable applications), class 42 (software services), and potentially other classes for nutrition information. Inspect mark holders, status, priority date, territories and actual goods/services. IPO search: https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark
2. Consider the existing Portiva health/nutrition app in relevant territories (UK, EU and any intended international markets) and professional similarity/opposition advice.
3. Verify company/business trading names, app stores, domains (.com, .co.uk, .app), social handles and any common-law use. **Domain registration availability has not been verified.** Even a free domain is not trade mark clearance.
4. Do not buy domains, file a trade mark, commission final logos, rename GitHub Pages or Stripe business, or sell under Portuva until cleared or founder expressly accepts a lawyer-reviewed risk.
5. Keep an alternative distinctive name shortlist available if Portuva fails clearance.
