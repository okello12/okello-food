Third-party data and software notices

Version 1 · 8 September 2026

Open Food Facts

Okello Food uses Open Food Facts when you deliberately look up a barcode or packaged product. The Open Food Facts database is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Individual database contents are subject to the Open Food Facts database-contents terms. Product images are generally supplied under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike terms as described by Open Food Facts.

Source: Open Food Facts. Database licence: ODbL 1.0. Okello Food is not affiliated with or endorsed by Open Food Facts.

The app records the Open Food Facts source on saved packaged foods when it can. Product information is community-maintained and may be incomplete or wrong; check the physical packet label when accuracy matters.

html5-qrcode

The barcode scanner uses the open-source html5-qrcode JavaScript library, delivered through jsDelivr and cached where possible for offline use. See the upstream project for its current software licence and notices.

Source: html5-qrcode project. Delivery network: jsDelivr.

GitHub Pages

The beta site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub provides the static hosting infrastructure; GitHub is not the nutrition-data provider and does not operate the Okello Food product.

No bundled commercial nutrition database

The current beta does not bundle a paid commercial food-composition database. Regional reference values and recipe estimates are being moved to source-level evidence records so the origin and uncertainty of each value can be audited.
