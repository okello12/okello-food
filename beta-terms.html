Adult beta terms

Version 1 · 8 September 2026

Pre-launch beta. These terms describe the current testing boundary. Final commercial terms must identify the operating legal entity and contact details before a paid or broad public launch.

Who may use this beta

The beta is intended for adults aged 18 or over. It is not intended for children's weight management, eating-disorder treatment, pregnancy care or clinical diet management.

What the app is for

Okello Food is a food-literacy and general-wellbeing tool. It helps adults record meals, understand approximate portions, calculate home recipes and review food information. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool or clinical service.

What the app does not do

Do not use it to diagnose, treat, monitor or manage disease; calculate insulin or medication doses; replace medical advice; or decide whether to start, stop or change treatment. If you need condition-specific nutrition advice, use an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Nutrition estimates

Food composition and portion values can be approximate, especially for soups, stews, restaurant meals, oil-heavy dishes and mixed recipes. Packet labels and your own weighed recipe usually provide stronger evidence than a generic database estimate. Missing source data may be shown as unavailable rather than zero.

Your device data

The beta has no Okello Food account or cloud diary. Your main diary is stored in your browser. Clearing site data, changing devices or losing the device can remove it. Use the backup tools if you want a recoverable copy.

Third-party services

Deliberate packaged-food searches and barcode lookups use Open Food Facts. The barcode scanner uses an open-source library delivered through jsDelivr. Those services have their own availability and terms. See Third-party notices.

Beta reliability

This is testing software. Features may change, estimates can be incomplete, and bugs may occur. Report material errors through the in-app beta feedback tools where available.

Privacy

See the beta privacy and device-storage notice for the current data flow and local-storage model.

Commercial status

No paid subscription or cloud service is created by these beta terms. Final consumer terms, operator details, cancellation/refund provisions and governing-law wording must be completed before commercial launch.
